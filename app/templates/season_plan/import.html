Bring a plan in from a spreadsheet: one row per week, plus optional sheets for the phases and milestones. Nothing is saved until you have checked the preview.

The format

Sheets weeks (one row per week: week, week_commencing, phase, block, event, break, theme, then a column per squad with its own theme, classroom, off_pitch, session_title, session_notes), phases and milestones. Only the week column is required.

Excel template (.xlsx) Weeks only (CSV) {% if plan %} Export this plan (.xlsx){% endif %}

Exporting, editing in a spreadsheet and importing again is a quick way to change a lot of weeks at once. Importing for a season that already has a plan updates it: weeks and phases are matched on their numbers; coaches' notes, linked sessions, pages and classroom sessions are kept.

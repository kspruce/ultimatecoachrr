The format

  • Each # Throw and catch line starts a section (made if there isn't one by that name yet).
  • Each ## Card 4 · Grip, pivot and fakes heading under it starts a topic in that section, titled with the words after the number. Everything under it, up to the next heading, is the topic.
  • A ## heading before a section's first card (such as “How to use these cards”) is kept as a draft note for coaches.
  • Uploading again adds new cards and moves cards already here (matched on title) into the section the file gives them. A section left empty by those moves is removed.

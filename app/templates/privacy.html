Privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

  • Your team decides what goes into Ultimate Coach. We keep it so your team can use it, and nothing else.
  • We don't sell data, show adverts or use tracking or analytics cookies.
  • Only members of your team can see your team's data, and what they see depends on their role.
  • Your team admins can download all of the team's data, or delete it, at any time.

Your team and us

Each team on Ultimate Coach is run by its own admins (usually its captains or coaches). The team decides who joins, what is recorded about its players and who can see it, so the team is responsible for that information. We store it and keep it safe on the team's behalf, and only use it to run the service for that team.

We are responsible for the information needed to run the site itself: accounts, sign-in and security records.

What we hold

Accounts Username, email address (optional), role in the team, and a scrambled (hashed) copy of the password, never the password itself. Superadmins also have a two-step sign-in secret and hashed recovery codes.
Player profiles Name, shirt number, position, gender (used for mixed-division line ratios), and anything else the team chooses to add: date of birth, height, weight, email, phone number, notes, goals and coach feedback.
Playing and training Game stats (who was on each point, passes, scores, turnovers), attendance and availability for sessions and tournaments, fitness test results and goals.
Team content Session plans, drills, plays, season plans, links to video clips and their notes, and scouting reports on other teams, which can include the names of opposing players.
Technical records Server logs with IP addresses, browser details, pages requested and errors, used to keep the site working and secure.

Why we use it

  • To provide the service your team signed up for: showing your team its plans, stats and squad, and sending emails you need, such as invitations and password resets.
  • To keep it secure: rate limits on sign-in, security logs, and investigating misuse.
  • To fix problems: error reports and logs.

We rely on the legitimate interests of your team and of us in running a team management tool, and on performing our agreement with your team. We don't use your data for marketing, adverts or profiling, and we don't sell it.

Young players

Teams may include players under 18. Teams should only add a young player's details with the agreement of a parent or guardian where that's needed, and only add what they need. Players under 13 should not have their own account unless a parent or guardian has set it up or agreed to it.

Who can see it

  • Your team's members, depending on their role: players see less than coaches and admins, and coaches choose what players can see in some areas, such as the season plan.
  • Anyone with a share link a coach creates: a shared session plan (including its attendance list), which the coach can turn off, or a shared clip and its notes, whose link stops working after the time the coach chose (7 days unless they pick up to 90).
  • Our site administrators, only to support teams, fix problems and keep the site secure. Their accounts need two-step sign-in.
  • Other teams never see your team's data.

How long we keep it

  • Your team's data is kept while the team uses Ultimate Coach.
  • Coaches and admins can delete players, accounts and anything else at any time, and team admins can delete the whole team under Team settings → Your data. Deleting is immediate.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data held about you, have it corrected or deleted, get a copy to take elsewhere, or object to or restrict how it's used. The quickest way is to ask your team's admins: they can change or delete your details and download everything the team holds. {% if contact %}You can also email {{ contact }} and we'll reply within a month.{% endif %}

If you're unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the data protection regulator, which in the UK is the Information Commissioner's Office.

Keeping it safe

The site only works over an encrypted connection, passwords are stored hashed, each team's data is kept separate, administrator accounts need two-step sign-in, and the database is backed up regularly.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top, and tell team admins about important changes.

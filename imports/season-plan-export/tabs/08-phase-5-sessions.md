# Phase 5 sessions: Nationals

31 July to 22 September 2028 · weeks 44-51 · UKU Nationals (\~5-6 Sep); Euros stretch goal (\~9-11 Sep)

## How Phase 5 works

**The shape:** a recovery week after Regionals and EUCS, three weeks to rebuild and peak, a taper week, then Nationals. No new material: everything is something the squads already know, done faster and sharper.

| Weeks | Stage | Intensity | Volume |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 44 | Recovery | Low | Low |
| 45 | Rebuild: fundamentals at game speed | Medium-high | Medium |
| 46-47 | Peak: high-intensity small-sided games and rehearsal | Highest | Medium |
| 48 | Taper | Short and sharp | Low |
| 49 | Nationals (and Euros) | Light session only | Very low |

- **Fitness:** specific high-intensity work peaks 2-3 weeks before Nationals (weeks 46-47), then a 1-2 week taper. Nothing is new: the repeated sprints have been built up in the 4v4 block since week 21, and the jumps are in the home programme. In the taper, cut the volume but keep the speed, so legs feel fresh and sharp. Pullers stop weighted-disc work a few weeks before Nationals (BFD S\&C; Season plan, Fitness).
- **Hands before legs:** CUSB found that after a summer break, their hands got worse faster than their legs, so their last sessions before EUCF had lots of throwing at high intensity (HN Case Studies, CUSB 2019).
- **Fresh for learning:** players learn badly when tired, so explain anything important before the hard work starts (HN Coach Corner, Planning Practices).
- **Scouting for Nationals:** CUSB spent the month before EUCF watching games of every likely opponent; we do a smaller version in weeks 45-46 (HN Case Studies, CUSB 2019).
- **Identity:** PoNY rallied around being the best defensive team in the country. Use the values from Herdlodge and the squad's own identity in the last briefings (HN Case Studies, Pony 2018).

**The 2s in Phase 5: the step-up camp.** Their competitive season ended at Regionals, but their development doesn't stop. Weeks 45-48 are a four-session camp built on each player's week 35 tests and mid-season one-to-one, run by the 2s captains with the skill leads. The aim is that every 2s player leaves with a measured improvement and a written plan for 2028/29, and players close to the 1s standard get targeted reps. Camp tests in weeks 45 and 48 (the two skill tests nearest each player's focus) measure the improvement; they are the 2s' own and separate from the club's eight mini-tests. Week 46 still has the season review and awards, after the camp session. Week 47 is camp session 3: the 2s play the 1s' rehearsal game, each with one thing to show and a 1s player or guest watching them. The 2s have nothing to taper for, so their week 48 session is a full one. Joint sessions stay open to everyone until the taper.

**Tests and focus time in Phase 5:** the week 45 mini-test is the first 20 minutes of a 30-minute skill floor. Weeks 45 and 46 keep the skill floor at game speed. Week 51 is the fourth full test round.

**Session lengths:** joint sessions are about 2.75 hours in weeks 45-46 and 2.5 in week 47; splits are about 2.5 hours; the taper sessions are 90 minutes or less. Each table says so.

**If the 1s don't qualify:** Phase 5 becomes an end-of-season block for the whole club: the recovery week, two weeks of fun games and a club mini-tournament, then the week 51 skill tests, reviews and awards. The 2s step-up camp runs as planned, and 1s players who want it can join it.

**Euros:** if the 1s qualify for Euros (\~9-11 Sep), confirm availability and travel in week 46 so it doesn't distract from Nationals.

## Week 44 · 31 Jul · Recovery

**Purpose:** rest legs and heads after two big weekends in a row.

- **One optional session:** throwing in pairs, mobility, and a social.
- **No split sessions.** Players log any niggles with the captains so they can be managed through the peak.
- **Captains' work:**
  - The 1s captains do the full Regionals and EUCS review: stats by line, film, and the top fixes for week 45.
  - The 2s captains do their season review and plan the end-of-season games and awards.
- **Tell players:** an easy week is part of the plan, not a break from it. Recovery is what makes the peak possible.

**What the games push players to do**

- **Fix game and conditioned 7v7 (joint):** the Regionals fix, with one rule. Nothing here is new; the question is: "What did Regionals show? What does this rule make us do faster?"
- **4v4 continuous (joint):** short games with full rest. At this point in the year it is for speed: every point at Nationals pace.
- **5-pull, Plan A (1s):** five pulls from the same start. Plan A, done quickly.
- **Focus games (2s):** each pitch plays the game for one focus area, and players choose their own. Everyone spends 40 minutes on the thing their tests say they most need, in a game. Ask each player: "What are you trying to show the 1s captains by week 48?"
- **Small-sided games with the break-throw bonus (2s):** the week 10 game, to finish. Players can feel how far they have come since December.

**Harder / easier**

- **4v4 continuous:** harder: stall 4; easier: stall 6.

**Coaching notes:** scouts are assigned to the likely Nationals opponents this week; cards are due in week 46. **Mini-test:** the first 20 minutes of the joint skill floor; it is the last of the eight. The 2s' camp test in the split is separate: it uses the two tests nearest each player's focus, so for many 2s players one of them is already done in the joint session.

**What the games push players to do**

- **3v3 and 4v4 on a 30 m pitch (joint):** three-minute games with full rest. Short and all-out, like Nationals points.
- **Short-stall 7v7 (joint):** stall 5, then stall 3, as in week 38. Early cuts, early throws.
- **Break-throw bonus 7v7 (joint):** the hardest version, at full spacing. It is the same game the club first played in week 10.
- **7v7 against each opponent's looks (1s):** the defence plays what the scouting card says. Plans A, B and C are rehearsed against the picture players will see.
- **1v1 ladders (2s):** winners move up a court, losers down. Everyone ends up playing someone of their own level, so every rep is hard. Ask: "Which cut, or which break, got you up a court?"

**Harder / easier**

- **3v3 and 4v4:** harder: a 25 m pitch; easier: a 35 m pitch.
- **Short-stall 7v7:** harder: stall 3 for the whole block; easier: stay at stall 5.
- **1v1 ladders (2s):** harder: the defender is told the cut; easier: the defender starts a step off.

**Coaching notes:** confirm Euros availability and travel this week if the 1s are in contention, so it's settled before the peak.

## Week 47 · 21 Aug · Peak: full rehearsal against the 2s and guests

**Learning points:** (1) Game-day routine exactly as at Nationals. (2) Switch plans on the captains' calls. (3) Reset after mistakes and stay present.

**Background (say two at most)**

- **This is the hardest week.** After it, everything gets lighter.
- **For the 2s:** you are the opposition. Play each scouted team's looks as well as you can; it's a chance to play against the 1s at full speed. Afterwards, each 2s player gets one piece of feedback on their development focus from a 1s player or guest.

**Coaching notes:** this is the last hard leg session; leave at least two days before the next one (Season plan, Fitness).

## Week 51 · 18 Sep · Season review

**Purpose:** show everyone how much they've improved, learn what to change, and keep players for next season.

**Joint session (about 2.75 hours)**

| Time | Activity | How it runs | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Warm-up | Herd standard warm-up and 4v3 mini | HN Practice Sessions; Kieran |
| 0:20 | Final skill tests | Five stations of 12 minutes, with 5 minutes for changeovers: the same four skill tests as in October, at trials and in May, plus the filmed 5-10-5. Protocols are in the Phase 1 tab | Season plan, Measuring improvement |
| 1:25 | Water and scores | Players enter their scores and look at their own October-to-September change | Season plan, Measuring improvement |
| 1:35 | Nationals review | 10 minutes: the four numbers, and three things to keep for next season | HN Coach Corner, Base Metric Analysis |
| 1:45 | Festive mixed games | Golden Arm, Honeypass, mixed 7v7 | IF; Kieran |
| 2:30 | Club awards | Most improved in each squad, spirit award, and the club's own awards | Plan |

**What the games push players to do**

- **Golden Arm and Honeypass:** the first games the club played in October, with the same mentor pairs. They end the season where it started, so players can feel the difference. Ask the pairs: "What can your partner do now that they couldn't in October?"

**After the session:**

- **One-to-one reviews:** short one-to-ones for every 1s and 2s player, using their October-to-September test curve, stats, two clips from the season and the mid-season focus.
- **Captains' review:** what worked, what didn't, and what to change in this plan for 2028/29. Use the player survey and the season's stats.
- **Off-season:** 1-2 months of low-intensity training 2-3 times a week, not a full stop (BFD S&C).
- **Recruitment:** Phase 1 of 2028/29 starts in October; plan the open skills sessions now (Season plan, Recruitment).

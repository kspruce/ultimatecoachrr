# Phase 5 sessions: Nationals

31 July to 22 September 2028 · weeks 44-51 · UKU Nationals (\~5-6 Sep); Euros stretch goal (\~9-11 Sep)

## How Phase 5 works

**The shape:** a recovery week after Regionals and EUCS, three weeks to rebuild and peak, a taper week, then Nationals. No new material: everything is something the squads already know, done faster and sharper.

| Weeks | Stage | Intensity | Volume |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 44 | Recovery | Low | Low |
| 45 | Rebuild: fundamentals at game speed | Medium-high | Medium |
| 46-47 | Peak: high-intensity small-sided games and rehearsal | Highest | Medium |
| 48 | Taper | Short and sharp | Low |
| 49 | Nationals (and Euros) | Light session only | Very low |

- **Fitness:** specific high-intensity work peaks 2-3 weeks before Nationals (weeks 46-47), then a 1-2 week taper. In the taper, cut the volume but keep the speed, so legs feel fresh and sharp. Pullers stop weighted-disc work a few weeks before Nationals (BFD S\&C; Season plan, Fitness).
- **Hands before legs:** CUSB found that after a summer break, their hands got worse faster than their legs, so their last sessions before EUCF had lots of throwing at high intensity (HN Case Studies, CUSB 2019).
- **Fresh for learning:** players learn badly when tired, so explain anything important before the hard work starts (HN Coach Corner, Planning Practices).
- **Scouting for Nationals:** CUSB spent the month before EUCF watching games of every likely opponent; we do a smaller version in weeks 45-46 (HN Case Studies, CUSB 2019).
- **Identity:** PoNY rallied around being the best defensive team in the country. Use the values from Herdlodge and the squad's own identity in the last briefings (HN Case Studies, Pony 2018).

**The 2s in Phase 5:** their competitive season ended at Regionals. They have end-of-season games and awards in weeks 45-46, and then join week 47 as the 1s' rehearsal opponents, playing the looks from the scouting cards. Joint sessions stay open to everyone until the taper.

**If the 1s don't qualify:** Phase 5 becomes an end-of-season block for the whole club: the recovery week, two weeks of fun games and a club mini-tournament, then the week 51 benchmarks, reviews and awards.

**Euros:** if the 1s qualify for Euros (\~9-11 Sep), confirm availability and travel in week 46 so it doesn't distract from Nationals.

## Week 44 · 31 Jul · Recovery

**Purpose:** rest legs and heads after two big weekends in a row.

- **One optional session:** throwing in pairs, mobility, and a social.
- **No split sessions.** Players log any niggles with the captains so they can be managed through the peak.
- **Captains' work:**
  - The 1s captains do the full Regionals and EUCS review: stats by line, film, and the top fixes for week 45.
  - The 2s captains do their season review and plan the end-of-season games and awards.
- **Tell players:** an easy week is part of the plan, not a break from it. Recovery is what makes the peak possible.

## Week 47 · 21 Aug · Peak: full rehearsal against the 2s and guests

**Learning points:** (1) Game-day routine exactly as at Nationals. (2) Switch plans on the captains' calls. (3) Reset after mistakes and stay present.

**What to tell players**

- **This is the hardest week.** After it, everything gets lighter.
- **For the 2s:** you are the opposition. Play each scouted team's looks as well as you can; it's a chance to play against the 1s at full speed.
- **Pressure:** practise the "what if" situations now so they feel familiar at Nationals (HN Coach Corner, Preparing for Pressure).

**Joint session: fun competition at peak intensity**

**Coaching notes:** this is the last hard leg session; leave at least two days before the next one (Season plan, Fitness).

## Week 48 · 28 Aug · Taper

**Purpose:** arrive at Nationals fresh and sharp. Cut the volume; keep a little speed.

- **One light joint session (about 90 minutes):** the game-day warm-up and the 4v3 mini, throwing, set pieces, a few short sprints, and short games.
- **1s split (about 60 minutes):** walk-throughs of Plans A, B and C, pulls, and endzone and Spread reps.
- **No conditioning.** Sleep, food and water matter more this week than extra training.
- **Tell players:** feeling a bit flat early in a taper is normal; the legs come back by the weekend.

## Week 49 · 4 Sep · UKU Nationals (\~5-6 Sep); Euros (\~9-11 Sep)

- **Light session early in the week:** game-day warm-up, throwing, walk-throughs, pulls.
- **Final briefing:**
  - Process goals and the squad's identity.
  - Scouting cards and lines.
  - The heat or wind plan.
  - Spirit intention (HN Coach Corner, Practicing Spirit).
- **At Nationals:** follow the event routine, with the sideliner recording the four numbers every point.
- **If Euros follows:** recovery and walk-throughs only between Nationals and Euros; no training.

## Week 50 · 11 Sep · Rest

No sessions. The captains collect the season's stats and send out the player survey.

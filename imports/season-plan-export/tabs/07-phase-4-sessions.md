# Phase 4 sessions: Regionals

19 June to 30 July 2028 · weeks 38-43 · UKU Regionals (\~18-19 Jul) and EUCS Summer Tour (\~25-26 Jul, 1s only)

## How Phase 4 works

**What changes:** no new structures. The playbook from Phases 2-3 is sharpened, scouted and rehearsed until each squad can switch plans on a call. Sessions get shorter and sharper: high intensity, a little less volume, and they finish on time.

- **Joint session:** the first hour is one fundamental refresher, chosen from the latest review; the rest is game-speed play and rehearsal.
- **Split session:** built around scouting and Plans A, B and C, with the 4v4 conditioning block kept.
- **The 4v3 mini** stays as the warm-up finisher every week and at every event.
- **Heat:** July sessions need water breaks every 20-25 minutes, shade for the sideline and sunscreen; shorten the conditioning block on hot days rather than skipping water (Plan).
- **Fitness:** keep the speed and agility work ticking over; the big peak comes 2-3 weeks before Nationals, in Phase 5. Hard leg sessions should not fall on consecutive days (Season plan, Fitness).

**Scouting**

CUSB put their 2019 EUCF result down to scouting: a team of scouts watched three or four games of each opponent, with two people watching the same game for different opinions, and wrote down every player's favourite throws and cuts. Then they trained how to escape the situations they didn't like (HN Case Studies, CUSB 2019).

- Each likely Regionals opponent gets two scouts from the squad, assigned in week 38.
- Scouts fill a one-page card: best players and their favourite throws and cuts; main offence and defence; what they do after a timeout; what they do in wind (HN Case Studies, Mooncatchers scouting sheet).
- Cards are presented in the week 40 split session, and printed for the sideline at Regionals.

**Plans A, B and C**

|  | 1s | 2s |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offence Plan A | Horizontal stack with the dominator | Vert from the back |
| Offence Plan B | Vert from the back or front | Vert from the front (3H) |
| Against a zone | Zone offence: around, through, over | Around and through |
| Endzone | Endzone; Spread against poaching defences | Endzone (Spread optional) |
| Defence Plan A | Person D, triangle mark, poach and clog | Person D with poach and clog |
| Defence Plan B | Bull zone; CRUMBLE to come out | Basic Bull zone |
| Wind / Plan C | Arrowhead with a line trap | Arrowhead |

**Triggers for switching (Plan):**

- **Defence:** switch after conceding two easy holds in a row, or when the opponent is scoring in fewer than five passes.
- **Offence:** switch after two turnovers in a row from the same problem.
- **Who decides:** the captains, at a timeout or between points. Plans are known before the game, so the call is one word (BFD, Game plans).

**Lines:** the 1s settle an O-line and a D-line by the end of week 40 (the 2s can if they want). The sideliner records O or D starts so stats split by line (HN Coach Corner, Base Metric Analysis).

**Mental skills:** big games bring nerves, and that's normal.

- **Process goals:** process goals that can be hit many times a game give feedback; outcome goals don't. For example, make them throw 10 passes before they score, or get 2 breaks every 6 points (HN Amsterdam Conference, Process over outcome).
- **Accept and refocus:** a flexible mind notices the nerves or the mistake, accepts it, and brings attention back to the next job, rather than trying to make the feeling go away (HN Coach Corner, Getting out of your head). The Inner Game of Ultimate calls the doubting, judging voice the Heckler and the trained, instinctive player the Hero: let the Heckler plan and review, and trust the Hero in games. Use a cue word such as "soft hands" or "patient and sharp" to get back to relaxed concentration (The Inner Game of Ultimate Frisbee).
- **Controllables:** "loose, but serious": focus on things you can control (HN Case Studies, Pony 2018).

## Week 38 · 19 Jun · Tour 3 review; breaking and cutting refresher; mid-season reviews

**Learning points:** (1) one fix from the Tour 3 review. (2) Cut early: the disc should move before the defence is set. (3) Every break throw goes straight into a continuation.

**What to tell players**

- **Why stall 3:** CUSB played many scrimmages at stall 5 or even stall 3. Their players hated it, but it made cuts start earlier and the disc move before the defence set up, which beats poaches and junk sets (HN Case Studies, CUSB 2019).
- **Process over outcome:** Regionals is the outcome; this week's goal is something we can hit many times a session, like two breaks every six points (HN Amsterdam Conference, Process over outcome).
- **Mid-season reviews:** everyone, 1s and 2s, gets a short one-to-one in the next two weeks (Season plan, The 2s).

## Week 39 · 26 Jun · Marking and switching marks; defence plans; 2s arrowhead in full

**Learning points:** (1) Triangle mark by default, flat mark on a call, changed straight away. (2) The mark's job carries on after the release: stop the give-and-go. (3) Know the defence plan and its trigger.

**What to tell players**

- **The mark:** stay on the balls of your feet, shuffle rather than lunge, and keep hands low and unpredictable. Take away the dangerous throw first, then close. After the throw, stop the give-and-go (Phase 1 weeks 7-9).
- **Flat mark:** on the call, flatten to take away the around break; everyone downfield knows the open side has changed (BFD).
- **Straight after a timeout:** expect the opponent to run a set play; we can double-team the reset or poach the nearest throwing lane for three seconds (HN Coach Corner, Time Outs).
- **2s, arrowhead:** a wall in front of the disc, the chase forcing towards the line, and a line trap with two tips once the disc reaches the sideline. Don't get outflanked (HN Flik, Arrow Head; Flik Zone Concepts 6).

## Week 40 · 3 Jul · Retention under pressure; offence against the defences we expect

**Learning points:** (1) Only throw to open players. (2) Against poaches and junk, move the disc before the defence sets. (3) 2s: choose the vert by call.

**What to tell players**

- **Escape what we don't like:** CUSB trained specifically to escape the situations they hated (poaches, junk zones, anything that stopped their flow), and so will we, using the scouting cards (HN Case Studies, CUSB 2019).
- **Poachy endzones:** call Spread and hit the open player at the back (Season plan, Structures).
- **After our own timeout,** expect a poach or a double team on the reset; the play is designed with that in mind (HN Coach Corner, Time Outs).

## Week 41 · 10 Jul · Game speed and full rehearsal

**Learning points:** (1) Everything at game speed. (2) Rehearse the Plan B and C triggers in a real game. (3) A reset routine after mistakes.

**What to tell players**

- **Nerves are normal:** don't try to make them go away. Notice the feeling or the mistake, accept it, and bring your attention back to your next job on the pitch (HN Coach Corner, Getting out of your head).
- **A reset routine:** after a mistake, one breath and one cue word, then straight to your next job. That quiets the Heckler and hands back to the Hero (Plan; based on HN Coach Corner, Getting out of your head, and The Inner Game of Ultimate Frisbee).
- **Loose, but serious:** focus on what you can control: your effort, your next cut, your mark (HN Case Studies, Pony 2018).

**Joint session (about 2.5 hours)**

**Split sessions:** full rehearsal: a real game with timeouts, line calls and scouting cards on the sideline, where the captains trigger Plans B and C at least once each. The 2s do the same with their plans. Keep it under 2.5 hours and finish with something fun.

**Coaching notes:** no new information this week. Make the plans feel simple.

## Week 43 · 24 Jul · EUCS Summer Tour (\~25-26 Jul)

**Purpose:** recover from Regionals and use EUCS well. Back-to-back weekends are hard on legs, so this is a recovery week, not a training week.

- **One light joint session (about 90 minutes):** mobility, throwing, the 4v3 mini, and short games.
- **Quick Regionals review (15 minutes):** the top two fixes only. The full review is in Phase 5 week 45.
- **Walk-throughs** for EUCS, and how it will be used:
  - If the 1s qualified for Nationals, EUCS is a Nationals rehearsal: play Plans B and C on purpose against strong teams.
  - If not, EUCS is the season's last big event; play to win and give everyone big minutes.
- **2s:** the 2s don't play EUCS, so their competitive season ends at Regionals. This week is an optional throwing and social session for them; their end-of-season games come in Phase 5.

Phase 5 starts with a recovery week (w/c 31 Jul).

# Theory reading guide

A reading plan for the 44 Herd theory cards (`herd-theory-cards.md`) and everything they draw on. Use it to write up each card properly before it goes out, and to get the ideas fresh in your own head before you coach them.

It has four parts:

1. **The write-up routine:** what to do for each card, in about 45 minutes.
2. **The pre-season read-through:** one theme a month from now until the season starts, so every source has been read once and the Phase 1 cards are finished before week 1.
3. **The in-season schedule:** every card in release order, with the week to write it, what to read and what to check.
4. **The library:** every source, where it lives, what it's best for and which cards use it.

Dates follow the 2027/28 season plan: week 1 is w/c 4 Oct 2027, and a card goes out on Discord a day or two before its session.

---

## 1. The write-up routine

Do this for each card the week **before** it's released.

1. **Re-read the draft card** (5 min). Note anything that feels thin or that you can't explain without notes.
2. **Read the sources** (15–20 min). Open each one on the card's Source line and read just the named section, not the whole thing. The "Read" column in part 3 lists them.
3. **Check the card against the source** (5 min). Is each key point right? Is anything missing that a player needs? Keep it to three points; extra detail goes in Next level.
4. **Do the diagram** (10 min). Pull it from the Ultimate Coach Figures folder or sketch it from the card's Diagram line.
5. **Check the links** (5 min). The drill links and videos open, and the Try it session still matches the weekly plan.
6. **Publish and schedule** in the theory section.
7. **One line for yourself.** Write down the one thing from the reading you want to see at training that week. That's your coaching cue for the session.

Tick the card off in part 3 when it's out.

## 2. The pre-season read-through (Nov 2026 – Sep 2027)

**Why start now:** 17 cards go out in the first ten weeks (weeks 1–10), three or more some weeks. That's too many to write in-season, so the plan is to read each theme once before the season and **finish the Phase 1 cards as you go** (marked ★ below). The later cards only need a draft check now; you write them up in-season using part 3, which gives you a second, shorter read just before you coach them.

About two to three hours a week is enough.

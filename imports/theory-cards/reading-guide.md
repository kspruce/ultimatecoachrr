# Theory reading guide

A reading plan for the 44 Herd theory cards (`herd-theory-cards.md`), the coaching sources behind them and the four Substacks in the Substack reading guide. Use it to write up each card properly before it goes out, and to get the ideas fresh in your own head before you coach them.

It has six parts:

1. **The write-up routine:** what to do for each card, in about 45 minutes.
2. **Six clashes to settle first:** where a Substack post disagrees with a card. Decide these before the card is finished.
3. **The pre-season read-through:** one theme a month from now until the season starts, so every source has been read once and the Phase 1 cards are finished before week 1.
4. **The in-season schedule:** every card in release order, with the week to write it, what to read and what to check.
5. **The library:** every source, where it lives, what it's best for and which cards use it.
6. **Substack posts by card:** the full list, with links.

Dates follow the 2027/28 season plan: week 1 is w/c 4 Oct 2027, and a card goes out on Discord a day or two before its session. ★ marks the Substack posts to read first for each card.

---

## 1. The write-up routine

Do this for each card the week **before** it's released.

1. **Re-read the draft card** (5 min). Note anything that feels thin or that you can't explain without notes.
2. **Read the sources** (15–20 min). Open each one on the card's Source line and read just the named section, not the whole thing. Then read the card's ★ Substack posts. The "Read" column in part 4 lists both.
3. **Write four lines per post** (5 min): *What it says / Where it agrees or clashes with our card / What we change / One drill or game.*
4. **Update the card** (5 min). Is each key point right? Is anything missing that a player needs? Keep it to three points; extra detail goes in Next level. Add each post you used to the Source line (for example *IF "When and Why Fakes Work"*), and put one Substack link at the end of Next level as "Read more".
5. **Do the diagram** (10 min). Pull it from the Ultimate Coach Figures folder or sketch it from the card's Diagram line.
6. **Check the links** (5 min). The drill links, videos and Read more link open, and the Try it session still matches the weekly plan.
7. **Publish and schedule** in the theory section.
8. **One line for yourself.** Write down the one thing from the reading you want to see at training that week. That's your coaching cue for the session.

Tick the card off in part 4 when it's out.

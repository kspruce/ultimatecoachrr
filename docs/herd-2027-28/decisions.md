## D2. Selection criteria for trials

**Recommendation.** Publish this, with the trial dates, before the Christmas break:

1. **Commitment comes first.** Say what each squad is signing up for before Trial 1: events, training, squad size and how playing time works in knockout games. A player who can't commit to the 1s' events is considered for the 2s.
2. **Four grades, each out of 5.**
   - *Skills:* the four skill tests at Trial 1 (hucking alley, Get 10, 1v1 cutting, catching). Each test is turned into 1-5 by where the score falls among everyone tested that day; the Skills grade is the average of the four.
   - *Throwing, cutting, defence:* graded in the trial games by one assessor per attribute, as the plan already says, and averaged over the three trials.
3. **Selection score:** the average of the four grades. Rank on it, then discuss anyone within about 0.3 of the line using the selectors' notes on decisions, competitiveness, spirit and coachability.
4. **Improvement breaks ties.** Between two players on the same score, the one who improved on more tests since October gets the place. Newcomers with no October score are not penalised.
5. **Everyone gets feedback and a development focus,** whichever squad they are in.

**Why.** It uses the tests the plan already runs, keeps the plan's one-assessor-per-attribute method, and makes skills a quarter of the decision, so "skills first" is visible without letting a test day outweigh three sessions of games. The athletic profile stays out of selection, as the plan says. If Herd enters mixed divisions, set the number of places by gender match-up first and rank within each group.

**Alternatives.** (a) Skill tests as a minimum standard only (for example, at or above the club median on two tests), with games deciding the rest. (b) Skills at 40% of the score, for a stronger "skills first" signal, at the cost of rewarding good test-takers.

## D3. The 2s-to-1s pathway criteria

**Recommendation.** Keep the proposed standard and add when and how it is checked:

- **Standard:** at or above the 1s squad's median on at least three of the four skill tests, plus the captains' judgement of game stats and attitude. The 1s medians come from the most recent full round (Trial 1 until week 35, then the week 35 round) and are published in numbers.
- **Two review windows:** after Tour 2 (week 32) and after the mid-season tests (week 36). At each one, the four captains look at every 2s player who meets the standard, or is one test short.
- **Any player can ask to be re-tested** on any of the four tests at the next mini-test.
- **Game evidence:** the handler tally and turnovers per touch from filmed 2s games, compared with the 1s' middle player.
- **Moving up means** training with the 1s' split for two weeks, then the next event. Check UKU's roster rules for moving a player between two teams of one club before promising a date.
- **The step-up camp** (weeks 45-48) sets each player's pathway numbers for 2028/29.

**Why.** The process goal is that at least two players move up. Without fixed windows, nobody is looked at until someone remembers.

**Alternatives.** (a) A looser standard: two of four tests plus game evidence. (b) Guarantee a look, not a move: the top two 2s players by test rank train with the 1s for a fortnight at each window, whatever their scores.

## D10. Get 10 scoring, and the length of the mini-test

**What changed in the plan.** The sessions now run Get 10 against both forces (the "confident break thrower" definition needs both), and every mini-test is 20 minutes with both tests in full, because two tests don't fit in 10 minutes for the whole club (review, finding 3).

**Recommendation.** Record Get 10 as two scores out of 10. For the goal "improves on 3 of the 4 skill tests", count Get 10 as improved if the total out of 20 goes up. Keep the 20-minute mini-test.

**Alternatives.** (a) Keep 10 minutes and run one full test per mini-test, alternating (cutting in weeks 6, 15, 27, 40; Get 10 in weeks 10, 22, 32, 45). (b) Keep 10 minutes and halve the reps, accepting noisier scores.
